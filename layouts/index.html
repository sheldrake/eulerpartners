Making consequential AI decisions

AI deployment in uncertain, complex environments.

When markets were complicated, expert analysis could map cause and effect and define a plan. In complex environments, cause and effect become visible only in retrospect — the technology moves faster than planning cycles can absorb.

  • Embedded in your team to design and execute a portfolio of safe-to-fail experiments
  • Instrument real-time feedback loops
  • Establish clear criteria for what to scale and what to retire
  • The understanding and know-how for your team to continue.

Agentic Governance & Operating Design

Bridging the gap between intent and execution.

Leaders need to evidence and steer AI activity — its potential value and its risks — in language the boardroom can understand and act on. We design measurement and governance drawing directly on scorecarding and stakeholder governance experience: indicators that show direction and momentum rather than static targets, and reporting that answers the questions the board is actually asking.

  • AI inventory, assessment, and compliance
  • Governance framework, scorecard and reporting cadence
  • Decision rights embedded in workflows, where humans sit in agentic loops
  • Workflow and decision-rights redesign
  • Implementation design

The Organisation as Organism

From mechanistic processes to adaptive, resilient systems.

Traditional frameworks treat organisations like machines. But when technology and markets shift faster than annual or quarterly cycles, the organisations that endure look more like living systems — sensing, learning, adapting. This is not a metaphor. It is a practice rooted in complexity science and biology.

We advise leadership teams on what this looks like in practice — how to structure and lead an organisation built to adapt.

  • Executive masterclasses
  • Understanding the implications for org design
  • Influence Scorecard
  • Wardley mapping
