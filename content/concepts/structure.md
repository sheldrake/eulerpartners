# [Concepts](/concepts/) / Structure

The enduring patterns formed by [relationships](/concepts/relationships/) among people, groups and institutions form the basic [social](/concepts/social/) structure of a society. In other words, social structure is an enduring emergent outcome of a [complex](/concepts/complexity/) system.

In [sociology](/concepts/sociology/), structures are defined in terms of rules and resources or sets of transformation relations that are organized as properties of social systems. (See [structuration theory](/concepts/structuration-theory/).)

Popular [software frameworks](/concepts/software-framework/), [platforms](/concepts/platform/) and [protocols](/concepts/protocol/) are structure.

In everyday conversation, use of “they” in referring to some remote or inaccessible third party can often be interpreted as a reference to structure. “What have _they_ done now?” “When are _they_ going to fix it?” “Why would _they_ do that?”
