# [Concepts](/concepts/) / Distributed ledger

Distributed ledgers use cryptographic techniques to ensure multi-party consensus, even when adversaries attempt to interfere, without central coordination.The _distributed_ part refers to it being a [distributed computing](/concepts/distributed-computing/) system. The _ledger_ part conveys that it maintains a collection of accounts in which transactions are recorded.

All [blockchains](/concepts/blockchain/) are distributed ledgers. Not every distributed ledger is a blockchain.
