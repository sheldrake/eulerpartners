# [Concepts](/concepts/) / Holon

A holon is simultaneously a whole and a part, an important concept in [systems thinking](/concepts/systems-thinking/). It's an evolving, self-organizing system (or phenomenon) composed of other holons. Their structures sit on the boundary of chaos and order (see [complexity](/concepts/complexity/)).

> ... the rules, or codes, which govern a [social](/concepts/social/) holon act not merely as negative _constraints_ imposed on its actions, but also as positive _precepts_, maxims of conduct or moral imperatives. ... Every holon will tend to persist in and asserts its particular patterns of activity. ... No man is an island — he is a holon. (Koestler, A. 1967)
