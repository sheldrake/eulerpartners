# [Concepts](/concepts/) / Platform

A platform may be hardware and/or software offering some valuable utility to draw others to develop hardware and/or software and/or services specifically compatible with it. This then deepens and extends the value of the platform. Examples include Apple iPhone, Google Android, ARM architecture, Salesforce, and Ethereum.

Open [protocols](/concepts/protocol/) can enable and possibly encourage [decentralization](/concepts/decentralized/). However, platform owners may pursue a strategy known as embrace, extend and extinguish. They come to centralize [governance](/concepts/governance/) of the protocol’s application through the platform’s [network](/concepts/networks/) effects, and impair interoperability to the disadvantage of competitors.
