# [Concepts](/concepts/) / Blockchain

A blockchain is a form of [distributed ledger](/concepts/distributed-ledger/) that organizes data into blocks and features an append-only structure, i.e. nothing can be edited or deleted after the fact.

The Bitcoin system was the first instance of a [cryptocurrency](/concepts/cryptocurrency/) blockchain, i.e. its design prevents Alice using the same single digital token to pay both Bob and Charlie (double-spending).

Ethereum is the first blockchain to feature a cryptocurrency and [smart contract](/concepts/smart-contract/) functionality.

There is a wide variety of blockchain designs.
