# [Concepts](/concepts/) / Networks

A network is a collection of interconnected things, e.g. roads, proteins, electrical components, computers, people, multiple species in an ecosystem.

Networks are observable in all life (see [ecology](/concepts/ecology/)).

> (We have) a new view of continual cooperation, strong interaction, and mutual dependence among life forms. Life did not take over the globe by combat, but by networking. (Margulis & Sagan, 1997)

Any observed network typically contains sub-networks and is itself a sub-network (see [holon](/concepts/holon/)). The more [complex](/concepts/complexity/) the network, the more resilient it is (see [variety](/concepts/variety/).)

Network science studies complex networks, and network theory is the study of graph representations of networks (see [social graph](/concepts/social-graph/)).
