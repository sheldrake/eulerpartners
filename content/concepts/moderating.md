# [Concepts](/concepts/) / Moderating

Moderating is the name we give the subset of [governance](/concepts/governance/) that [structures](/concepts/structure/) participation in a [community](/concepts/community/) to facilitate cooperation and prevent abuse (Grimmelman, J. 2015). It is a feature of all community, digitalized or not. It’s part and parcel of being [social](/concepts/social/).

To date, [social network](/concepts/social-networking/) moderating is taken on by a small minority of users and / or paid moderators focused on global content removal.

[Decentralized](/concepts/decentralized/) social (dsocial) networking is more likely to encourage all participants to play their part, amplifying and attenuating behaviours, locally and contextually. The diligent digitalization of human [identity](/concepts/identity/) will be critical.

Community entails [conversation](/concepts/conversation/) and other interactions. Moderating may then be framed as conversation about the conversation (first-order [feedback](/concepts/feedback/) loop), and governance more broadly as conversation about the conversation about the conversation (second-order feedback loop).
