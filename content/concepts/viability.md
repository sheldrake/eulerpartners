# [Concepts](/concepts/) / Viability

If a system can continue to reproduce itself (i.e. exist) over time, it is referred to as a viable system.

Viability is a function of, amongst other things, [feedback](/concepts/feedback/) and [variety](/concepts/variety/), and just as for these concepts it emerged from the field of [cybernetics](/concepts/cybernetics/).

Every viable system is contained in other viable systems (see [holon](/concepts/holon/)).

The cybernetician Stafford Beer developed a rigorous appreciation for viability through his development of the Viable Systems Model.
