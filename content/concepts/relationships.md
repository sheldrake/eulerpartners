# [Concepts](/concepts/) / Relationships

In [social](/concepts/social/) terms, a relationship describes the way in which two or more people are connected.

Relationships are required for the creation and transformation of information, the expansion of organizational [identity](/concepts/identity/), and the accumulation of wisdom.

Relationships are formed with information exchange, i.e. including [conversation](/concepts/conversation/), between identifying / identifiable entities in identifying / identifiable organizings.

See [social graph](/concepts/social-graph/).
